As a child, before my tenure as a world-renowned artist and musician, I spent my time creating masterpieces of an entirely different kind. Read on to discover just a teeny portion of the incredible, astounding, and mind-blowing things invented by me.

Unicorn Boat
Similar to a rowboat, but with four strategically-placed, magnetic indentations to secure your unicorn's hooves. No more unicorns falling overboard when raiding the neighboring kingdoms!

Bean Bag Chair Collection Chamber
A storage solution for when you have too many bean bag chairs. It houses your beanbag chairs and keeps them easily accessible for when guests drop by.
Simply deposit the chair in the top of the tube and it falls to the bottom where it can be easily retreived through the door at the bottom. Can stack up to 6 bean bag chairs!

Brain Encasement Gel
Easily injected around the brain, it cushions and absorbs shock. No need for football helmets anymore!

Grub Crater
Do you hate taking out the trash? Do you hate people breaking into your house all the time? Then the Grub Crater is for you! A 6' x 10' moat that surrounds your home or office is filled with grubs. They eat your garbage...as well as any pesky would-be intruders. Thanks, Grub Crater!
